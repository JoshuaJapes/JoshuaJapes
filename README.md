### Joshua Penny — Senior Cyber Threat Intelligence Analyst

Senior CTI Analyst working cross-sector, focused on tracking adversary infrastructure and turning open-source reporting into actionable intelligence and validated detections.

I use this space to build public YARA/KQL detection libraries, experiment with LLM-assisted CTI workflows, and archive my published threat research. 

Off grid, I’m an avid Fantasy and Sci-Fi reader, devouring the works of John Gwynne, Joe Abercrombie, James Islington, and much more. To balance this out, I maintain a regular presence at the local gym and walk my energetic Boxer dog twice a day.

#### Core Focus
1. **Adversary & Campaign Tracking:** End-to-end intrusion analysis across ransomware affiliates, initial access brokers, and social-engineering ecosystems.
2. **Detection Engineering:** Translating threat reporting into production-grade YARA rules and Microsoft Defender / Sentinel KQL hunting queries.
3. **Infrastructure Pivoting:** Mapping C2 networks, phishing kits, and bulletproof hosting using passive DNS, certificate telemetry, Shodan, UrlScan, and other available tools.
4. **AI-Enabled CTI Workflows:** Building structured agent skills and Python pipelines for automated TTP extraction, STIX 2.1 modelling, and indicator enrichment.
